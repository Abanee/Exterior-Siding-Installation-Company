Guiding Philosophy

A better exterior starts with better decisions.

Great residential architecture is never accidental. Successful siding transformations depend on an intentional harmony of material weight, surface texture, color harmony, and corner geometry.

01. Material

Balancing thermal performance, lifecycle durability, and authentic grain character.

02. Texture

Controlling light reflection through smooth matte reveals or tactile wood textures.

03. Color

Composing primary wall tones with high-contrast architectural fascia and window trim.

04. Profile

Selecting between traditional horizontal lap, modern vertical lines, or panel grids.

05. Trim

Framing architectural openings with crisp mitered corners and sealed casing lines.

06. Style

Honoring the home's original silhouette while infusing contemporary elegance.

Architectural Typologies

Tailored siding solutions for distinct home styles.

Different residential architectural styles call for distinct siding widths, profiles, and trim proportions.

Modern Architecture

Clean Lines & Minimal Profiles

Characterized by expansive dark geometric fiber cement panels, flush hidden fasteners, and expansive floor-to-ceiling glass reveals.

Recommended: Smooth Fiber Cement Panels with 8mm reveal channels.
Traditional Architecture

Balanced Proportions & Classic Lap

Timeless horizontal bevel siding with pronounced shadow courses, complemented by historic 4-inch casing and crown window headers.

Recommended: 6-inch Cedarmill Lap Siding in Warm Slate.
Modern Farmhouse

Warm Textures & Vertical Definition

Dramatic vertical board-and-batten siding with dark matte standing-seam accents and contrasting black window trims.

Recommended: 12-inch vertical panels with 1.5-inch battens.
Contemporary Architecture

Bold Material Mixing & Natural Accents

Dynamic interplay between organic Western Red Cedar vertical slats and deep architectural charcoal cladding.

Recommended: Mixed Medium - T&G Cedar paired with Matte Ironstone.
